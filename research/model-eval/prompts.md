# Test prompts

Each `##` section is one prompt. The runner sends the body of each section as a single owner
message, with no other context.

## landlord-email

Can you draft an email to my landlord? The kitchen tap has been leaking for a week and nobody has
come to look at it.

## fridge-dinner

I've got eggs, half an onion, some spinach, cheddar and a bit of leftover rice. What can I make for
dinner?

## plan-saturday

I've got nothing on this Saturday and the weather looks nice. Help me plan the day.

## reminder

Remind me to call Mum at 6.

## headphones

Should I buy the expensive headphones? They're £350 and the ones I have still work fine.
