# Ground truth for the example transcript

The owner's lines are the only evidence. The assistant's lines can guess, suggest, or be wrong.

## What the owner said

1. They are moving to Leeds and pick up the keys on the 20th.
2. They asked for a moving checklist.
3. They asked for a note to their landlord giving a month's notice.
4. Their sister Ana's birthday is on the 18th.
5. They said the assistant cannot book things.

## Unknown: the owner never said

- Whether the landlord note was sent.
- Whether a van is booked. The assistant claimed it booked one; the owner contradicted it.
- The owner's gender or pronouns.

## Expected memory changes

- The seed entry m1 ("Owner lives in London") is out of date and should be updated: the owner is
  moving to Leeds and picks up the keys on the 20th.
- Reasonable new entries: Ana's birthday is on the 18th; the move date.

## Open loops a good writer lists

- Send the landlord notice.
- Book a van.
- Ana's birthday on the 18th.
- The moving checklist.
